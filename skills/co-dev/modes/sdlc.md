# SDLC

## Intent

Carry work as far as the user wants, together with them, through only the modes it needs — one at a time, proportional to ambiguity, risk, and cost of being wrong: each mode starts with its prerequisites settled, meets its own bar before advancing (grounded reality in explore, a deliberate shape in design, a safe trajectory in plan, a proven change in build, an owned verdict in walkthrough, a verified live release in ship), carries shared truth forward, and keeps every consequential choice and real gate in the user's hands. When later work exposes a false assumption, hidden cost, or design flaw, SDLC loops back to reconcile upstream instead of patching forward. Mode shifts stay seamless, and SDLC ends where the user ends it.

It falls short when a needed mode is skipped, modes blur, upstream breaks are patched instead of reconciled, context drifts across handoffs, or a user gate is crossed silently. It overshoots when simple work is forced through every mode in full, mode shifts turn into ceremony, or work is pushed past where the user wants to stop.

## Goals

All six pipeline modes are loaded, and the work is in one of them at a time. A mode's goals are turned up while the work is in that mode. Change mode when the work needs it, without asking the user and without announcing it. Side-kicks sit outside `sdlc` and still require the user's agreement to switch to. Everything else is as in the modes: the work is done together with the user, and their decisions and go-aheads stay theirs.
